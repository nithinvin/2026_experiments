# 7. Cheat Sheet

[Previous: All the game modes](06_game_modes.md) | [Back to index](README.md)

## Numbers to remember

| Thing | Number |
|---|---|
| Team size | 5 |
| Rounds to win a standard match | 13 |
| Side swap | After round 12 |
| Round clock | 100 seconds |
| Plant time | 4 seconds |
| Spike countdown | 45 seconds |
| Defuse time | 7 seconds, saved at 3.5 |
| Starting money each half | 800 credits |
| Round win / base loss | 3,000 / 1,900 credits |
| Per kill / per team plant | 200 / 300 credits |
| Max credits | 9,000 |

## Jargon

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ace** | One player kills all five enemies in a round |
| **Clutch** | Winning a round as your team's last survivor |
| **Eco** | A round where you save money |
| **Flank** | Attacking from the side or behind |
| **Lurk** | Playing alone away from your team to catch rotating enemies |
| **Peek** | Stepping out from cover to look for enemies |
| **Rotate** | Moving from one site to another |
| **Retake** | Winning a site back after a plant |
| **Post-plant** | The part of a round after the Spike is planted |
| **Trade** | Killing the enemy who just killed your teammate |
| **One-tap** | A kill with a single headshot |
| **Smoke** | An ability that blocks vision |
| **Flash** | An ability that blinds |
| **Ult** | Ultimate ability |
| **Orb** | Map pick-up that gives ultimate points |
| **Save** | Avoiding a fight to keep your gun for next round |
| **Fake** | Pretending to attack one site, then hitting another |

## Quiz yourself

1. All five attackers are dead, but the Spike is planted with 20 seconds left. Has the round ended?
2. The Spike has 6 seconds left and you've just reached it. You have no defuse progress. What should you do?
3. You have 2,500 credits. Can you buy a Vandal and heavy shields?
4. You want a quick game with normal Spike rules and shopping. Which mode fits?
5. Who can pick up a dropped Spike?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. No. Defenders still have to defuse it, and they have plenty of time.
2. Run. A full defuse takes 7 seconds, so you can't finish.
3. No. That's 2,900 + 1,000 = 3,900 credits.
4. Swiftplay.
5. Only attackers.

</details>

## How sure are these facts?

- **Well confirmed:** agents, map names, recent patch changes and the Hush release all come from Riot's site.
- **From recent guides:** Spike timings and most mode rules come from 2026 third-party guides, not from Riot directly.
- **Less certain:**
  - **Prices:** come from an October 2025 list, and only the Warden is confirmed from a 2026 Riot patch. The Outlaw was changed in patch 13.01, and that change wasn't checked. Guides from different years disagree on some others.
  - **Buy phase:** most guides say 30 seconds, but one recent guide says 12 seconds.
  - **Deathmatch:** guides disagree on lobby size (12 or 14).
  - **Economy bonuses and the role list:** the economy figures come from older guides. Agents are grouped by role from general knowledge of the game, because Riot's agent list page doesn't show roles (only Hush as a Duelist was checked directly).
- **Map pool changes constantly.** Riot's map page lists 13 maps: Abyss, Ascent, Bind, Breeze, Corrode, Fracture, Haven, Icebox, Lotus, Pearl, Split, Summit and Sunset. Patch 13.08 (October 2026) moved Bind and Corrode into the Competitive and Deathmatch queues, and Ascent and Split out. The full current queue wasn't confirmed, so check in the game.
